NAVIGATION

The frame slider, playback, mouse wheel, jump buttons, and Go To all
move through the video. Frame numbers in the UI are 1-based.

CLIP LIMITS

After you split a video, preview and work modes clamp navigation to that
clip. Playback stays inside the clip. Slider zoom is nested inside the
clip range (or the full video in Entire video mode).

If you type a Go To frame that is outside the current clip, RIPPLE
offers to preview the clip that contains it.

ENTIRE VIDEO

The Entire video chip unlocks the slider so you can watch the whole
movie. Annotations are read-only (stitched from every clip JSON plus
the unsaved working clip). Optical flow, SAT, and new tracks stay off.

PREVIEW VS WORK

Selecting a clip chip is preview only: cheap, no flow load. The banner
has Switch to this clip (commit: save previous, load this clip’s flow)
and Back / Entire video.

REVIEW, FOLLOW, ONION-SKIN

N / P jump to the next or previous review issue (interior gap,
occlusion start, or an unoptimized seed-only track). [ / ] jump
anchors on the selected track.

View → Follow selected track during playback keeps the active particle
centered while you play. View → Onion-skin previous frame blends the
previous frame under the current one so jitter and drift are easier
to see.
